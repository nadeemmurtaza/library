# Indie Sleaze UI design style

Flash photography, messy nightlife imagery, condensed type, black backgrounds, low-fi textures, and deliberately unpolished cool.

## Design overview

## History and origins

A fact-checked account of where this visual language came from, with links to the references used.

Indie Sleaze is a retrospective label for a period of deliberately messy nightlife imagery, direct flash, skinny type, low-resolution blogs, club flyers, and indie-electro fashion. CARI dates the source period from the early 2000s to early 2010s and records Dalia Barillaro coining the name in October 2018, well after the scene itself.

## Design principles and implementation

Use these principles to write a focused AI UI design prompt instead of asking for a vague visual treatment.

Use black, dirty white, flash blue, nicotine beige, and occasional lipstick red or electric accent with harsh photographic contrast. In a daisyUI theme, use flash blue for `primary`, lipstick red for `secondary`, and assign dark charcoal, near-black, and black to `base-100`, `base-200`, and `base-300`.

Choose direct-flash parties, candid bands, bathroom mirrors, street style, venue ephemera, blurred movement, and low-fi camera artifacts.

Use high image density and compact captions, preserving blunt whitespace or solid black around lineups, credits, and calls to action.

Use flash cuts, jittered photo swaps, abrupt scroll reveals, timestamp flicker, and short handheld-feeling transitions.

Use crooked photo rectangles, contact sheets, tape, scribbles, timestamp blocks, thin rules, and minimally styled form elements. In daisyUI themes, set the `--radius-*` variables to `0` or `0.25rem`.

Use condensed sans-serif for lineups and navigation, mixing in plain serif or handwriting accents while keeping the overall treatment deliberately unpolished.
