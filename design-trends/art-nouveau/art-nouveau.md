# Art Nouveau UI design style

Flowing botanical lines, decorative frames, elegant lettering, natural motifs, and integrated ornament.

## Design overview

## History and origins

A fact-checked account of where this visual language came from, with links to the references used.

Art Nouveau emerged in the 1890s as an international attempt to create a modern, unified art spanning architecture, interiors, posters, type, and objects. Its regional forms differed, but flowing plant-like lines, asymmetric composition, and integrated ornament became common signals; World War I largely ended its first period of prominence.

## Design principles and implementation

Use these principles to write a focused AI UI design prompt instead of asking for a vague visual treatment.

Choose cream, sage, muted teal, plum, dusty rose, and antique gold in softened botanical harmonies rather than bright digital primaries. In a daisyUI theme, use muted teal or plum for `primary`, sage or dusty rose for `secondary`, and assign cream, pale parchment, and warm botanical gray to `base-100`, `base-200`, and `base-300`.

Use elegant portraiture, flowers, insects, vines, stained glass, and integrated line illustration with a consistent hand-drawn ornamental finish.

Keep density medium, concentrating detail in frames and corner ornament while leaving the center calm enough for reading and action.

Draw vines and whiplash curves along their paths, open floral frames, and dissolve imagery gently as if revealed through layered print.

Use whiplash curves, tendrils, petals, elongated arches, oval frames, and intertwined organic borders with few straight mechanical edges. In daisyUI themes, set the `--radius-*` variables between `0.75rem` and `1.5rem`.

Reserve decorative period lettering for names and short headings, then use an elegant readable serif with generous leading for all substantive copy.
