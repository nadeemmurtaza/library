# Cottagecore UI design style

Pastoral imagery, florals, handmade textures, gentle serif type, warm domestic details, and romantic rural nostalgia.

## Design overview

## History and origins

A fact-checked account of where this visual language came from, with links to the references used.

Cottagecore is a contemporary internet label for an idealized rural domestic life, with precursors in pastoral art, craft revival, and back-to-the-land culture. The named tag spread on Tumblr around 2018 and surged during 2020, when lockdowns intensified interest in baking, gardening, handwork, and imagined escape; its nostalgia can conceal the labor and inequality of actual rural histories.

## Design principles and implementation

Use these principles to write a focused AI UI design prompt instead of asking for a vague visual treatment.

Choose warm cream, sage, dusty rose, butter yellow, faded blue, and soil brown with soft floral accents and no synthetic neon. In a daisyUI theme, use sage or dusty rose for `primary`, butter yellow or faded blue for `secondary`, and set `base-100`, `base-200`, and `base-300` to warm cream, linen, and pale soil beige.

Use pastoral landscapes, gardens, baked goods, craft, linens, cottages, and candid community moments in warm natural light.

Keep density medium and leisurely, grouping useful detail into labeled notes while allowing photographs and stories generous room.

Use subtle breeze, page lifts, stitched-line draws, gentle object placement, and seasonal fades with quiet handcrafted timing.

Favor scalloped edges, floral frames, fabric labels, baskets, ribbons, rounded plaques, and small imperfect domestic motifs. In daisyUI themes, set the `--radius-*` variables between `0.75rem` and `1.5rem`.

Use a gentle literary serif for reading, add restrained handwriting for recipes or notes, and keep labels warm but never overly ornate.
