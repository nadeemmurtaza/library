# Heroic Realism UI design style

Idealized naturalistic figures become monumental symbols of strength, labor, unity, and progress in the legible, emotionally direct language of twentieth-century propaganda art.

## Design overview

## History and origins

A fact-checked account of where this visual language came from, with links to the references used.

Heroic Realism is a broad and politically charged label for idealized, legible depictions of workers, soldiers, leaders, and national bodies. It overlaps with Soviet Socialist Realism and with visual propaganda used by fascist regimes, so it should not be treated as a neutral unified school; current design usually borrows its monumental scale, low viewpoints, rays, and declarative type while separating those devices from their original ideology.

## Design principles and implementation

Use these principles to write a focused AI UI design prompt instead of asking for a vague visual treatment.

Use banner red, warm gold, cream, cobalt, earth, and steel tones in broad modeled areas, with radiant light reserved for the promised goal or collective horizon. In a daisyUI theme, use banner red or cobalt for `primary`, warm gold for `secondary`, and build `base-100`, `base-200`, and `base-300` from cream, pale earth, and muted steel-gray surfaces.

Commission painterly realist scenes of workers, farmers, soldiers, athletes, scientists, or citizens idealized against industry, agriculture, construction, or space, and retain historical context when quoting propaganda.

Keep copy low and rhetorical, pairing one concise slogan with a monumental image and moving dates, sources, or interpretation into a plainly separated information region.

Use slow banner unfurls, marching parallax, rising light, sequential crowd reveals, and weighty forward motion rather than playful or decorative effects.

Favor ascending triangles, radiating suns, broad banners, monumental plinths, strong diagonals, and simplified industrial silhouettes that reinforce collective scale. In daisyUI themes, set the `--radius-*` variables to `0` or `0.25rem`.

Set short statements in heavy condensed capitals or a forceful slab, maintain uncompromised legibility, and avoid imitating a specific political script or insignia without relevant context.
