# Corporate Memphis UI design style

Flat vector scenes with bright solid colors, geometric environments, and simplified people with exaggerated proportions.

## Design overview

## History and origins

A fact-checked account of where this visual language came from, with links to the references used.

Corporate Memphis is a critical nickname for flat, friendly vector illustration used by large technology companies. A major reference point is Alegria, created by Buck with illustrator Xoana Herrera for Facebook in 2017; its abstract people, bright shapes, and scalable scenes spread widely, while the “Corporate Memphis” label connected that wave—loosely and sometimes sarcastically—to the color and geometry of 1980s Memphis design.

## Design principles and implementation

Use these principles to write a focused AI UI design prompt instead of asking for a vague visual treatment.

Use bright brand primaries and soft secondary fills against a pale neutral background, keeping skin and clothing colors inclusive and clearly separated. In a daisyUI theme, use the brightest brand hue for `primary`, a softer contrasting fill for `secondary`, and assign pale cream, lightly tinted neutral, and soft gray to `base-100`, `base-200`, and `base-300`.

Create flat vector people with exaggerated limbs, simplified faces, geometric props, and workplace or service scenarios tied directly to the message.

Keep density medium to low, letting each scene explain one concept and avoiding decorative characters around already complex workflows.

Animate simple character gestures, floating props, and scene assembly with friendly easing while keeping the product interaction itself direct.

Use soft geometric environments, rounded rectangles, circles, and abstract plant or furniture forms with consistent flat outlines. In daisyUI themes, set the `--radius-*` variables between `0.75rem` and `1.5rem`.

Choose an approachable geometric sans-serif with open counters, use strong sentence-case headings, and keep the illustration more expressive than the type.
