# Seapunk UI design style

Turquoise water, dolphins, shells, early CGI, chrome, tropical graphics, and playful aquatic internet surrealism.

## Design overview

## History and origins

A fact-checked account of where this visual language came from, with links to the references used.

Seapunk began as both an internet joke and a small music-and-image scene. DJ Lil Internet coined the word in a 2011 tweet, after which turquoise oceans, dolphins, shells, 3D grids, Windows-era graphics, and aquatic fashion circulated rapidly through Tumblr and music communities before elements were absorbed into mainstream pop imagery.

## Design principles and implementation

Use these principles to write a focused AI UI design prompt instead of asking for a vague visual treatment.

Use turquoise, aqua, seafoam, cobalt, purple, and chrome with tropical gradients and bright watery highlights. In a daisyUI theme, use turquoise or aqua for `primary`, cobalt or purple for `secondary`, and set `base-100`, `base-200`, and `base-300` to pale seafoam, light aqua, and watery blue-gray.

Choose dolphins, shells, waves, tropical plants, early CGI creatures, reflective objects, and surreal ocean-photo composites.

Use medium to high visual density with concise copy, placing essential commerce or event information on opaque aquatic panels.

Bob objects, ripple surfaces, rotate early-CGI forms, trail bubbles, and shift underwater light with playful internet-era timing.

Favor bubbles, waves, shells, chrome rings, splash contours, fins, and soft early-three-dimensional primitives. In daisyUI themes, set the `--radius-*` variables between `0.75rem` and `1.5rem`.

Use playful rounded or digital display type with a simple sans-serif body, keeping small text dark and crisp against bright water imagery.

### Cybercore UI design style

A 2020s nostalgic remix of late-1990s and early-2000s digital culture combines saturated blue and silver with retro anime, obsolete devices, legacy software, and ethereal celestial symbolism.

### Cyberdelia UI design style

Cyberculture merges with psychedelia through acid color, fractals, kaleidoscopic imagery, early 3D type, and rave-era digital optimism.

### Frutiger Aero UI design style

Glossy blue-and-green visuals combine skeuomorphic interfaces, glassy controls, water, bubbles, open skies, nature, and optimistic technology.
