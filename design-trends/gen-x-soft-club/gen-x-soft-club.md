# Gen-X Soft Club UI design style

Late-1990s and early-2000s urban futurism softens Y2K gloss through muted color, minimalist typography, blurred photography, and a sterile-meets-organic mood.

Gen-X Soft Club is a retrospective consumer-aesthetic classification rather than a name used consistently during its source period. CARI places it from the early 1990s to mid-2000s and credits Sloane Angel Hilton with the label, describing a polished, translucent, nature-tinged calm that developed as technology and wellness were sold as softer parts of everyday life.

## Design principles and implementation

Use these principles to write a focused AI UI design prompt instead of asking for a vague visual treatment.

Use desaturated blue, seafoam or olive green, beige, tan, concrete gray, and black, binding photography and surfaces with cool color washes instead of candy-colored Y2K chrome. In a daisyUI theme, use desaturated blue or seafoam for `primary`, olive for `secondary`, and assign beige, cool light gray, and concrete gray to `base-100`, `base-200`, and `base-300`.

Choose airports, train platforms, city skylines, concrete interiors, plants, and natural-looking portraits; apply blur, bloom, bokeh, vignettes, Lomo-like cross-processing, or bleach-bypass effects without erasing the subject.

Keep density low to medium, letting one atmospheric image and a short message dominate while secondary navigation reads like quiet transit or album metadata.

Use slow crossfades, soft-focus shifts, restrained light leaks, passing-window motion, and short vector traces that evoke travel and club visuals without glossy spectacle.

Favor clean rectangular crops, thin route lines, small circles, understated technical marks, and softened photo edges, balancing sterile geometry with natural textures. In daisyUI themes, set the `--radius-*` variables between `0.25rem` and `1rem`.

Use a neutral Helvetica-like neo-grotesque, align it like urban wayfinding, and pair compact labels with spare lowercase or sentence-case headlines; keep tracking measured and effects minimal.
